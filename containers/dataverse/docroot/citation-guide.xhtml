Academic Citation Standards

Formal citation specifications, persistent identifier resolution protocols, and bibliographic exchange standards for publications and research datasets cataloged in OpenScience.ph.

Anatomy of an OpenScience.ph Citation

Every accepted manuscript, working paper, program archive, and dataset published on OpenScience.ph generates a standardized citation designed for seamless academic credit, scholarly discoverability, and permanent verification.

[Author/Creator], [Year], "[Title of Publication or Dataset]", [Global Persistent Identifier (N2T ARK URL)], OpenScience.ph, [Institutional Steward/Publisher], [Version].

Required Elements:

  • Author / Principal Investigator: Family name and given name of all contributors.
  • Year of Deposition: Year the record was formally registered or published.
  • Title: Full scholarly title of the manuscript, catalog, or research collection.
  • Persistent Identifier: The globally resolvable Archival Resource Key (e.g., https://n2t.net/ark:14373/x3...).
  • Repository Context: OpenScience.ph (stewarded by OBRA Incorporated).
  • Version State: Designates DRAFT VERSION or formal archival release (e.g., V1).

Major Scholarly Citation Formats

When referencing OpenScience.ph records in journal articles, dissertations, policy reports, or conference proceedings, format citations according to the target style guide:

APA (7th Edition)
Ang, N. (2019). Korea Pinoy International Film Festival: First Filipino International Film Festival in South Korea [Data set]. OpenScience.ph. https://n2t.net/ark:14373/x3WUEKPQ
Chicago Manual of Style (17th Edition, Author-Date)
Ang, Nash. 2019. "Korea Pinoy International Film Festival: First Filipino International Film Festival in South Korea." OpenScience.ph. https://n2t.net/ark:14373/x3WUEKPQ.
IEEE Style
N. Ang, "Korea Pinoy International Film Festival: First Filipino International Film Festival in South Korea," OpenScience.ph, 2019. [Online]. Available: https://n2t.net/ark:14373/x3WUEKPQ.
Harvard Reference Format
Ang, N., 2019. Korea Pinoy International Film Festival: First Filipino International Film Festival in South Korea. OpenScience.ph. Available at: <https://n2t.net/ark:14373/x3WUEKPQ>.

Reference Management & Formats

On every dataset and publication page, the Cite Publication dropdown allows one-click export into automated citation managers:

RIS Format (.ris)

Universal standard compatible with Zotero, Mendeley, and EndNote. Automatically parses creators, publication year, repository space, and the persistent ARK link.

BibTeX Format (.bib)

Standard structured format for LaTeX and Overleaf manuscripts, preserving precise field attributes and URL resolvers.

Persistent Identifiers (ARK)

OpenScience.ph assigns Archival Resource Keys (ARKs) to all deposits under Name Assigning Authority Number (NAAN) 14373.

Global N2T Resolution
https://n2t.net/ark:14373/x3[ID]

ARKs prevent link rot by routing through the global Name-to-Thing (N2T) resolver hosted by the California Digital Library. If storage nodes relocate, the persistent identifier remains permanently resolvable.
